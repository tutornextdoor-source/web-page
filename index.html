OUR STORY

Meet the sisters next door.

Tutor Next Door was founded by Ashwini, a high schooler in Monroe Twp, NJ, with her younger sister Naina helping from day one. It all started with what Ashwini needed as a kid.

When I was younger, it was hard to find a tutor who could actually help me. I jumped from group classes, tutoring schools, and private mentors each year. In the end, it was my family who was able to guide me, because they actually understood me.

For the past two years, I’ve been tutoring younger kids myself. I plan something new every week to make it click, and I’ve experienced how much it means to have someone patient on your side.

Many tutoring services are so focused on growing their business that they lose sight of their students. That’s why I started Tutor Next Door, to give more kids the supporting and understanding community I had.

Ashwini Narang, Founder11th grade · Monroe Township, NJ
QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is Tutor Next Door a registered NJ nonprofit?

Yes. Tutor Next Door is a registered nonprofit in New Jersey.

Does it cost anything?

No. There’s no fee for families.

How do you keep kids safe?

Sessions only happen in public places, preferably the public library. Every tutor is trained before their first session, and we talk with parents first, before any match is made.

Who are the tutors?

Local high schoolers in grades 8–12, trained before they start and matched to kids in their area.

What if there’s no tutor in my town yet?

Text us anyway. We’re looking for Town Leads to start new locations, and your interest helps us get there.

What does a Town Lead do?

A Town Lead brings Tutor Next Door to their own town: recruiting friends as tutors and helping local families get matched.
